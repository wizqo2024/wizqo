## ❓ FAQs About Nursery Class Worksheets

1. Are printable worksheets good for nursery kids?
Yes. Used in moderation, they build focus, hand control, and problem‑solving — especially when paired with play and conversation.

2. What subjects should nursery worksheets cover?
A healthy mix: literacy, math, art, mindfulness, and logic activities.

3. How often should kids do worksheets?
Start small — 10–15 minutes a day is plenty. Consistency matters more than length.

4. Can worksheets replace playtime?
No. Worksheets should complement, not replace, play. The best learning happens when both are balanced.

The Architecture of Capitals

Capitals are for important things! Our worksheets help young learners move beyond scribbling to formal orthographic structure. By practicing the A-Z capital set in isolation and alphabetical strings, children build manual coordination and character recognition speed. This practice is the essential precursor to writing names, proper nouns, and starting every sentence they will ever record. It turns the page into a rewarding workout that builds self-confidence and school-readiness. This is the symbolic foundation of literacy and effective communication. Early Writing Milestones

  • Stroke Accuracy: following the correct 'outside-in' or 'top-down' path for all 26 capitals.
  • Size Regularity: maintaining consistent height across the alphabet.
  • Grammatical Awareness: identifying when a 'big letter' is required in a sentence.

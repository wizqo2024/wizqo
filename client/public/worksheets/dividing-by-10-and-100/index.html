Moving the Decimal Point

Dividing by 10 or 100 doesn't require a long division algorithm - it just requires a simple shift! Our worksheets teach students the 'leftward shift' strategy, showing how the decimal point moves one or two places to represent a 10-fold or 100-fold decrease in value. This understanding of base-ten mechanics is a critical precursor to scientific notation and percentage math, helping students visualize the scale of numbers with ease.

Arithmetic Skills Target

  • Mental Shortcuts: Eliminating the 'mental tax' of calculation through pattern recognition.
  • Place Value Intuition: Seeing how digits 'decrease' in rank when we divide by ten.
  • Scientific Foundation: Preparing for metric unit conversions (like millimeters to centimeters).

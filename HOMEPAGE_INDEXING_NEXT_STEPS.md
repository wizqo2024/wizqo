## 🔍 WHY IT'S NOT SHOWING IN SEARCH YET

Since Google can crawl it but it's not appearing in search, there are a few possibilities:

### Possibility 1: Not Indexed Yet (Most Likely) ⏳
**Status:** Crawled ≠ Indexed

Google crawls pages first, then decides whether to index them. There can be a delay between:
- **Crawling** (Googlebot visits the page) ✅ You have this
- **Indexing** (Google adds it to search results) ❓ Check this

**How to Check:**
1. Go to Google Search Console
2. URL Inspection Tool
3. Enter: `https://wizqo.com/`
4. Check status:
   - ✅ "URL is on Google" = Indexed (should appear soon)
   - ⚠️ "Discovered - currently not indexed" = Needs more signals
   - ❌ "Crawled - currently not indexed" = May need request

### Possibility 2: Indexed But Not Ranking 📊
**Status:** Indexed but low authority

If it's indexed but not showing, it might be:
- **Low domain authority** (new site)
- **No backlinks** pointing to homepage
- **Low search volume** for your keywords
- **Competition** from established sites

**How to Check:**
1. Search for: `site:wizqo.com`
2. If homepage appears = ✅ Indexed (just not ranking)
3. If homepage doesn't appear = ⚠️ Not indexed yet

### Possibility 3: Indexed But Needs Time ⏰
**Status:** Recently indexed, needs time to appear

Even after indexing, it can take:
- **1-7 days** to appear in search results
- **2-4 weeks** to start ranking
- **Longer** for competitive keywords

### 2. Create Pinterest Account + 10 Pins (1 hour) 🎨

2. **Create 10 Pins Using Canva (free):**

     5. "Addition Worksheets Free"
     6. "Subtraction Worksheets Free"
     7. "Times Table Worksheets"
     8. "Fractions Worksheets Free"
     9. "Reading Worksheets Free"
     10. "Writing Worksheets Free"

3. **Pin Format:**
   - **Title (on pin):** "Free [Topic] Worksheets"

   - **Link:** Direct link to relevant worksheet page
   - **Image:** Show worksheet preview + colorful design
